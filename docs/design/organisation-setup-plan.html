01

The shape

Ask only what the first photo needs. Import what they already keep. Learn the rest from the first racks. Never block scanning on a form.

Same on the phone and on the portal. The setup state lives on the server. Whichever the admin opens first after approval shows the container: a card over the product (full screen on a phone), one step at a time, a short title and one line of help, only Back and Next at the bottom. No step bar, no numbering. Flat, plain forms: a mandatory field carries a red star after its label, an optional one carries nothing, and the last step lists what is still needed.

Only the admin sees it. The admin finishes setup when the organization is created and invites people afterwards, so a technician who signs in walks straight into the app. Afterwards the same sections are editable under Profile > Organization settings, and the container can be walked through again from there.

02

Required: five steps

01

Your organization

Required
Asked
  • Short code, country, time zone. The code goes on labels, reports and the app sign-in.
  • Primary contact name and email: the person RackTrack writes to about the account.
  • Optional: industry, website, phone, logo.
Why

Every scan, ticket and report carries the organization name and code, and every timestamp is shown in its time zone.

02

Datacentres, then spaces and racks

Required
Asked
  • Each datacentre as a facility record: address line 1, city, country and time zone required; region, postcode, facility code, provider, opening hours, access notes and coordinates optional.
  • Spaces in each: name, kind (hall, floor, room, row, cage), and a rack count. Floor, room, row and facility ID optional.
  • Racks per space: a number. Names come later, scanned or imported.
Why

A scan starts by choosing a space, so it only has that space's racks to match against. The address gives the building; GPS stops there, since there is no signal indoors and racks are 60 cm apart.

03

People

Required
Asked
  • One approver per datacentre. Name and email. Can be the admin.
  • Ticket contact. Defaults to the approver. More people (on-site, escalation, facilities, security) are optional.
Why

Every change to the record carries one name. Every ticket reaches a person.

04

Rules

Required, one tap
Shown, then accepted
  • Approve before write. Never delete.
  • Tickets go to the rack contact, then the site contact.
  • Photos kept 90 days. Racks 42U, counted from the bottom.
Why

These behaviours exist already. Showing them once makes them a choice, not a hidden default. All can be changed later.

05

Review and finish

The last step: what is done, what is still required, and the optional sections still empty, each with a way back. Finish closes the container. The same list stays on the settings page as the Remaining panel.
