Installing RackTrack on your iPhone

RackTrack isn't in the App Store yet — it's a beta, so it arrives through Apple's testing app, TestFlight. Two emails, one free download, about five minutes. This guide covers the install, and how to get updates afterwards.

Before you start

  • An iPhone — RackTrack runs on iOS 13 and later. TestFlight itself needs a newer iOS than that, so if the App Store won't let you install TestFlight, that's the reason.
  • Your Apple ID email — the address you actually sign into the App Store with. Your invitation is tied to it. If you gave us a work alias that forwards elsewhere, tell us; it won't work.
  • Internet. Wi-Fi or cellular, both fine.

The install

  1. Look for the invitation email

    It comes from Apple, subject line along the lines of “You're invited to test RackTrack.” Inside is a View in TestFlight button and a redeem code — a short string of letters and numbers. Either one gets you in; the button is easier.

    Not there? Check spam and any Promotions tab before asking us to re-send — it lands there more often than not.

  2. Install TestFlight from the App Store — do this first

    Open the App Store on your iPhone, search TestFlight, and install it. It's free, made by Apple, and it's the thing RackTrack installs through.

    Do this before tapping anything in the email. Tapping the invite without TestFlight installed just sends you to a webpage that tells you to go install TestFlight.

  3. Open the email on the iPhone itself

    This is the step people get wrong: the invite has to be opened on the phone you're installing on. Clicking it on a laptop does nothing useful. If the email only lives on your desktop, forward it to yourself and open it on the phone.

    Tap View in TestFlight. TestFlight opens on the RackTrack page.

    If the button doesn't work: open TestFlight yourself, tap Redeem in the top corner, and type the code from the email by hand.

  4. Accept, then tap Install

    TestFlight shows you what you're joining and asks you to accept. Then a blue Install button — tap it. RackTrack downloads and lands on your home screen like any other app, with a small orange dot beside the name marking it as a beta.

  5. Open RackTrack and sign in

    Launch it from the home screen — you don't need to go back through TestFlight. First run asks permission to use the camera, which RackTrack needs to scan racks. Say yes, or scanning won't work.

Sign in with the invited address. TestFlight matches your invitation against the Apple ID signed into the phone. If your iPhone is signed into a personal Apple ID but the invite went to your work address, TestFlight won't find it — and the error it gives you won't say so. Tell us which address to use and we'll re-issue.

Getting the latest version

RackTrack updates often, and beta apps don't update themselves through the App Store. New builds arrive through TestFlight instead.

  1. Turn on automatic updates once

    Open TestFlight → tap RackTrack → switch on Automatic Updates. New builds then install on their own and you can forget this section exists. This is the recommended setup.

  2. Or update by hand when you want

    Open TestFlight. If a new build is waiting, RackTrack shows an Update button — tap it. Apple also emails you when a build goes out.

  3. Read “What to Test”

    Each build comes with notes on the RackTrack page in TestFlight saying what changed and what we'd like you to look at. Worth ten seconds before you dive in.

Builds expire after 90 days. Apple's rule, not ours. When one lapses, RackTrack stops opening and TestFlight says the build has expired. Nothing is broken and you've lost nothing — open TestFlight and install the current build. If there isn't one, tell us and we'll ship it.

Telling us something's wrong

Beta feedback goes further than a message in a thread, because it arrives with the device, the iOS version and the exact build attached.

  • Screenshot anything odd — take a screenshot while RackTrack is open, then share it. iOS offers to send it as TestFlight feedback and it reaches us with the diagnostics attached.
  • Or from TestFlight — open TestFlight → RackTrack → Send Beta Feedback.
  • What helps most: what you tapped, what you expected, what happened instead. A screenshot of a wrong number beats a description of it.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
No invitation email Check spam and Promotions. Still nothing — confirm with us which address we sent it to.
The invite link opens a webpage, not TestFlight TestFlight isn't installed yet, or you opened the email on a computer. Install TestFlight on the iPhone, then reopen the email there.
“This invitation is no longer valid” The invite expired or is tied to a different Apple ID. Ask us to re-send to the right address.
RackTrack won't open — build expired Open TestFlight and install the current build.
App opens, screens are blank or error Not your phone. RackTrack talks to a server on our side — tell us and we'll check it.
Camera does nothing Settings → RackTrack → turn Camera on. Easy to decline on first launch.

One thing to know about the live data. RackTrack's photo scanning works anywhere you have internet. The live switch screens — ports, cables, trace — read your actual equipment through a server on our network. If those screens are empty while scanning works fine, it's our server, not your phone.
