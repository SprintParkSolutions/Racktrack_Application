Network side · the switches

Decides how much the tool learns by asking the devices directly.

Read-only access

Done
SNMP read-only · v2c community or v3 user

You gave us this, so the D-Link DGS-1210-52 and both TP-Link TL-SG2428P read fully. No write access is used, ever.

Have it: ports, status, VLANs, addresses on all 3 managed switches.

Neighbour announcements

Switch on
LLDP transmit + receive · every port · both ends

On for only a handful of links today, which is why just 6 cables are confirmed. It is a passive advertisement, safe to enable.

You get: which cable joins which two ports, proven by both switches.

Address table readable

Check
MAC forwarding table + ARP · comes with read access

This is how a port is named with the PC or laptop on it. It ships with the read access, so just confirm it is not filtered.

You get: the host on each port, matched to its IP.

Firewall opened for reading

Switch on
Sophos XGS 116 · SNMP read-only

The firewall speaks SNMP but is closed today, so it lands as a blank box (U17, 0 ports). Open read access and it joins the record.

You get: the firewall with its WAN + LAN ports, not an empty slot.

The two unmanaged switches

Add / manual
DGS-1024C (24) + DGS-1016D (16) = 40 ports · no management

These have no brain to answer anything, over any protocol. They stay photo-only unless replaced with managed units, or entered by hand.

To fill them: swap for managed, or we type them from your notes.

Rack side · labels and stickers

Decides how much the camera reads, and whether a device ties back to your own records.

Model & serial close-ups

Photo
sharp close-up · sticker not hidden by cables

Managed switch models now read over the network. For the unmanaged switches and the firewall, the model and serial need a close photo of the faceplate, not the wide shot.

You get: exact model and serial on the devices SNMP can't name.

Asset tags

Only if used
your ID sticker · barcode / QR / printed · unique per NetBox

An asset tag is your own inventory label on a device (say SP-00123). If your kit carries them we can capture them. If it does not, there is nothing to read and a labelling scheme has to come first.

If they exist: we read them by close-up, or take a tag→device list.

Cable colour coding

Done
blue = uplink · grey = end device (your scheme)

Already in place, and the camera reads it. That is how uplinks are told apart from desktop runs without asking anything.

Have it: cable colour on every socket the camera can see.

Patch-panel numbering

Helps
printed port numbers on each of the 4 panels

Clear numbers let each of the 168 panel sockets line up with the record on your side. Without them we still show the panel, just cannot name each port.

You get: named panel ports, so a wiring path reads end to end.
