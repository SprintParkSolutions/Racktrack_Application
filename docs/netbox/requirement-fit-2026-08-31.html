The one thing they care about most

They most want this: use the photo plus the switches to work out, and double-check, which cable plugs into which port.

Photo + switch, cross-checked

The photo finds the cables; the switches name what is on the other end. We line the two up. This works.

Proving both ends

A cable is fully proven only when both devices confirm it. Simple, unmanaged gear cannot, so those rest on the photo alone.

Cisco's version

They mentioned a Cisco-only way (CDP). We do not support that yet. It only matters if they have Cisco gear.

Video vs photo

They pictured a video sweep. We work from a single photo. Fine for a demo, but not the video they described.

What to fix first

In order. The first two give the most for the least effort.

1

Prove both ends of each cable

This is their favourite feature, and it is only half working.

Do: ask their network team to switch on the setting that makes each switch announce its neighbours. Then both ends of a cable confirm each other.

2

Finish the wiring records in NetBox

Decides whether the handover is actually useful to them.

Do: complete the part of the export that writes cable and patch-panel wiring properly.

3

Read the asset stickers

On their wish list, lower priority.

Do: read the ID label from a close-up photo, or take a simple list from them.

The honest summary: almost everything they asked for already works, and the model number is now solved over the network. We can demo a real rack end to end. The three things to be upfront about are proving both ends of a cable, finishing the wiring records, and asset stickers.
