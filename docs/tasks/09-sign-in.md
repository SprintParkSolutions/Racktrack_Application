# Sign-in and the front of the app

**SPRTMS-1640** · Sprintpark Rack Track Management System

## What this is

The first screen anyone sees.

## Why we did it

It was sized from the browser window rather than from its own box, so on some phones the button sat below the fold.

## What we built

- A logo, the product name, two fields and a button. Nothing else.
- Sized from its own box, so the button is on screen on every phone.
- The line under the name now reads: Physical Infrastructure Intelligence for Data Centers.
- The raised, soft-shadowed styling is gone, replaced with a flat treatment, and the page uses the full width of the device.

## How to check it

1. Open the app on a small phone. The button should be visible without scrolling.

## What is not done

Nothing outstanding.
