Please re-test these. Each line says what was actually wrong, not just that it was changed.

#What you reportedWhat was wrongWhat we changedStatus
1 Analysing screen: the percentage does not show Bigger than it looked. The analysing screen kept a black background from the old dark design, while everything drawn on it had already moved to the new light design. The title, the step text, the progress bar and the percentage were all dark text on a dark background - invisible, not missing. The background is now light, so the whole panel reads properly. The 3D rack animation was invisible for the same reason and is back too. FIXED
2 Guide bot: after "pick a device" nothing tells you to tap it in the rack The guide jumped straight from the device dropdown to "find the port". Nothing ever asked you to look at the photo, so the step was missing rather than unclear. Added a step: "Now find it in the photo." It moves on when you tap anywhere on the rack image, so you do not have to hit the device exactly. FIXED
3 Guide bot: after the port number it should jump to the port part of the report The report opened at the top, with the port detail further down and no sign it was there. When you have just found a port, the report now opens at the port section. It still opens at the top when no port has been found. FIXED
4 Downloading the report does not work on the phone app
(also reported as: a message appears instead of the PDF)		 Two separate faults, either of which alone would break it. One: the download used a command from a different app framework, which the phone app has no handler for - so the tap genuinely did nothing at all. Two: the PDF was sent marked "display this" rather than "save this", so even when it did open, the phone showed it instead of saving it. The download now uses the phone app's own browser handler, and the file is sent marked as a download. FIXED
5 Topology: Switches, Patch Panels and Servers counts are wrong The scan labels devices "Switch" and "Patch Panel", but the topology screen was checking for "switch" and "patch_panel". Those never match, so the counts read zero no matter what was found. The same fault also gave every device the wrong icon and the wrong position in the diagram. All 20 places that compared these labels now ignore capitals and spacing. Expect the topology diagram to look noticeably different, not just the counts. FIXED
6 Report: remove the "Models read" section and review the Switches and Routers card Both were summary tiles at the top of the report. Both tiles removed. The report header now shows Devices and Ports in use only. Every device's make and model is still listed in full further down - nothing was lost. FIXED
7 Report: the Back/Close button is hard to see It was a dark button on a nearly black bar with a faint outline, and it turned light when you hovered, which made it look like a different control. It is now the one light control on that bar, larger, rounded, and it carries the word Close next to the X. FIXED
8 Keep the bot in the black and white theme The guide bot was a flat drawing that did not match the rest of the app. Replaced with the new 3D robot, converted to black and white to match the app. It is also 71% smaller than the file we were given (3.2 MB down to 0.9 MB) and only loads when you open the guide, so it does not slow the app down. FIXED

These are not being fixed, and here is the honest reason for each.

#What you reportedThe answerStatus
9 Report sharing: there is no Slack Slack was removed on purpose. The decision was to keep Microsoft Teams and Outlook and drop Slack, so its absence is correct rather than a fault. Teams sharing is a separate open item - see #17. BY DESIGN
10 JPEG format missing in the two-rack setup JPEG is accepted. We checked the upload directly: it explicitly allows JPEG in three forms, plus PNG, WEBP and HEIC. Two likely explanations - either the video slot was used by mistake (that one only takes video, correctly), or the photo was refused by the image-quality check and the wording read like a format error. Please tell us the exact message you saw and we will chase whichever it was. NOT A FAULT
11 Why is there no VR and AR? Because it has not been built. There is no VR or AR feature in the app and none is hidden or broken - nothing is missing that was supposed to be there. The 3D rack and topology views are ordinary on-screen 3D, not headset features. If VR or AR is wanted, it is new work to be scheduled, not a bug. NOT BUILT
12 Devices detected does not match the device count in the report Confirmed, and it is a difference in what each number means. The screen counts only devices you can select and inspect - switches, patch panels, routers, gateways, firewalls and PDUs. The report counts all equipment in the rack, which also includes servers, UPS units, power units, storage and anything unidentified. Both are correct for their own purpose. A decision was taken to leave this as it is for now. LEFT AS IS
