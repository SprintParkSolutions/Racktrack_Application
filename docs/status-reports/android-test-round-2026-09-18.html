What is new, and what to test

A test build for Android. This page says what changed, what to try, and what a correct answer looks like, so you can tell a fault from something we have not built yet.

18 September 2026Android 1.0 (61)demo.racktrack.aiBuild cd925d6

The short version

This round is about one idea: the app stops guessing. Where it used to quietly pick an answer, it now either proves the answer or says it does not know. Everything below is a way of checking that.

Photograph the rack→ Compare with the record→ Admin hands it to someone→ They go and look→ Admin approves→ Written down

Two places, not one. The first two steps happen in the phone app. Everything an admin decides now happens in RackTrack Approvals, a separate page in the browser at demo.racktrack.ai/approvals/. The app opens it for you: there is an Approvals entry for admins, and a "Track this check" link right after you send one. Same sign-in, same data. If you are looking for the approvals screens inside the app, they are gone on purpose.

Before you start

Install. Build 1.0 (61) has gone out through Firebase App Distribution, the same way as every earlier build, so you should have it already. It is signed with the same key, so it installs over the one you have. Android will warn you it is not from the Play Store; that is expected for an internal build.

Where it points. This build talks to demo.racktrack.ai. It is our demo system, not a customer's. You cannot break anything a customer owns.

Take one account from the table below and keep it. What you can do depends on your role, so two people sharing one account will confuse each other's results.

What to test

Twelve things, in order. Each one says what to do, and what a correct answer looks like. If you see the "wrongly" line instead, that is worth reporting.

  1. Check your role does only what it should

    Everyone

    Sign in with your own account and try something your role should not allow. A technician should find no way to approve anything. An auditor should find no way to change anything at all.

    Right

    The action is either not offered, or it is refused with a sentence saying who may do it. Both are correct answers.

    Report if

    You can do something your role should not allow, or a refusal is a code rather than a sentence. This is the most valuable thing on the list.

  2. Photograph a rack

    Technician

    Open the app, scan a rack, and wait for the picture to come back with boxes drawn around the devices.

    Right

    Each device has a box round it and a shelf number. A patch panel is a patch panel, a switch is a switch.

    Report if

    A box is drawn round nothing, two boxes overlap on one shelf, or the shelf numbers do not follow the rack.

  3. Run a drift check and look at who it goes to

    Technician

    On that rack, run a drift check. Before you send it, look at the blue "Goes to" card.

    Right

    It names a person, their job title and their email, taken from the record. If the record names nobody, it says the admin will choose. Either answer is correct.

    Report if

    It names nobody and does not explain why, or it names somebody who has nothing to do with that rack.

  4. Send it, and check nothing was written

    Technician

    Send the check to the admin. Then look at the record for that rack.

    Right

    The app says it was sent. Nothing has changed in the record. Sending is not writing.

    Report if

    Anything at all appears in the record before an admin has approved it.

  5. Count the decisions

    Admin, in Approvals

    Press Track this check after sending, or open Approvals from the app. The browser opens RackTrack Approvals. Find your check and count how many things it asks you to decide.

    Right

    One per device, not one per port. A rack with nine devices and a hundred and seventy ports asks for nine decisions. The ports are listed underneath their switch and say they follow it.

    Report if

    You are asked to decide hundreds of things on a fresh check, or a port can be decided on its own.

  6. Try to approve something nobody has looked at

    Admin, in Approvals

    Pick a difference and try to approve it straight away, without handing it to anybody.

    Right

    It is refused, and the reason is that it has to be assigned first. This refusal comes from the server, so it holds however you press it.

    Report if

    It lets you approve, or the refusal is a number or a code rather than a sentence.

  7. Hand the whole rack to one person

    Admin, in Approvals

    Use the whole-rack action to hand everything still waiting to one person in one move.

    Right

    One action covers everything still waiting. A real ticket is raised and the person named gets it.

    Report if

    It raises one ticket per port, or it silently skips things that were waiting.

  8. Read a switch, and see which box the app thinks it is

    Admin, in the app

    On a rack you can reach over the network, add a switch on the Network step and let it be read. Then open Review.

    Right

    Under each switch, one plain sentence saying why that box was chosen, and one word for how sure it is: Confirmed, Probably, Possibly or Not identified. Where two boxes cannot be told apart, it says so and says what would settle it. It does not choose one.

    Report if

    It picks a box without saying why, or says "Confirmed" about something nobody confirmed.

  9. Confirm a box, and see what changes

    Admin, in the app

    Pick the box a switch actually is, then press the confirm button for that one switch.

    Right

    Only after confirming does that switch's real model, serial number and ports attach to that box. Before that, the report says those details are a proposal. Confirming one switch does not confirm any other.

    Report if

    Saving the list also confirms things, or confirming one switch changes the state of another.

  10. Find your way to Approvals from the app

    Admin

    From the app, open Approvals. Then go back and open an old bookmark or link that used to point at the approvals screen inside the app.

    Right

    Approvals opens in your normal browser, already signed in as the same person. An old in-app link lands on a short page saying Approvals has moved, with one button across. It is never a blank screen or a "page not found".

    Report if

    It asks you to sign in again, opens a blank page, or an old link dead-ends.

  11. A rack with no record system behind it

    Technician

    Run a drift check as somebody whose organisation has no NetBox or ServiceNow connected, if you have such an account.

    Right

    A plain panel saying there is no record to check against yet. An admin is offered a way to connect one; anybody else is told to ask their administrator. It is an explanation, not a red error.

    Report if

    You get a red error, a spinner that never ends, or a message with a status code in it.

  12. Photograph the same rack again

    Technician

    Take a second photo of a rack where you confirmed a switch, and let it be read again.

    Right

    The switch you confirmed still shows its box, and the wording says it was matched from an earlier check. You should not have to confirm it twice.

    Report if

    Your confirmation is forgotten, or a box you said was wrong comes back.

Not faults

These are deliberate. Please do not report them; if you disagree with one, say so as an opinion rather than a fault.

  • A technician cannot confirm which box a switch is. Only an admin can. The way somebody at the rack gets involved is that the admin assigns them the item and they get a ServiceNow ticket.
  • Two identical switches with identical cabling are not told apart. The app says so instead of choosing. That is the correct answer, not a gap.
  • A device is never deleted. Something in the record but not in the rack is reported, never removed.
  • A model the app only guessed is not written. It is shown as "probably" and stays out of the record.
  • On some racks the camera can only see about half the sockets, because the bottom row is hidden behind cables. Those sockets read as unknown, which is honest rather than wrong.
  • A rack the customer typed in by hand is not matched to yet. Everything in it reads as new. That is the piece being built now.

How to report something

Four lines is enough:

  • The rack - its RK- code from the top of the screen.
  • What you did - the step number above, if it was one of them.
  • What you expected, and what you got. A screenshot beats a description.
  • Roughly when - the time helps us find it in the logs.

If the app refuses something and the reason is a plain sentence, that is usually the app working. If the reason is a number, a code, or nothing at all, that is worth reporting even if the refusal itself was right.

The one thing worth breaking. Try to make the app state something it cannot prove. If you can get it to say a box is a certain switch when it has no good reason, or to write something you did not approve, that is the most useful fault you can find this round.
