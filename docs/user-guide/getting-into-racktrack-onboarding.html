Getting into RackTrack

There are only two ways in: someone invites you, or you create your company’s account. Work out which one you are, follow the steps on that side, and you will be scanning in about five minutes.

Read this first - it is where almost everyone gets stuck

Rule 1 · Your email decides your path

Only a @gmail.com address can create an account by itself. If your address is @yourcompany.com, the sign-up screen will refuse it.

That is not a fault - it means you are meant to be invited instead. An invitation accepts any address, work email included.

Rule 2 · The email code lasts 60 seconds

When RackTrack sends you a six-digit code, it is valid for one minute.

Open your inbox before you ask for the code, so you can read it the moment it lands. If the minute runs out, tap Resend and use the fresh one straight away.

Rule 3 · Nothing happens automatically

RackTrack does not email invitations, and nobody is alerted when a new company signs up.

A person has to send you the link, and a person has to approve a new organization. If you are waiting, message them - waiting quietly will not move it along.

Where to open RackTrack

Any device · fastest

Open demo.racktrack.ai in your browser. Nothing to install, and it works the same on a laptop and a phone.

iPhone / iPad

The RackTrack team sends you a TestFlight invitation. Install Apple’s free TestFlight app first, then RackTrack from inside it.

Android

The team sends you an APK file. Open it and allow the install when your phone asks.

Two ways in - pick your side

Path A

Someone sent you a link

This is how nearly everyone joins, including leaders and directors on a work email address.

  1. Open the link you were sent. The top of the page names what you are joining - for example “Join Acme · London as Member”. Check it is the right team and the right role.
  2. Your email is already filled in and cannot be edited. The invitation was issued to that exact address, which is how RackTrack knows the link is yours.
  3. Choose a username and a password. The password needs all five at once: 8 characters, an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit, and a special character such as ! or #. The meter under the box names the one thing still missing.
  4. Tap Join. That is it - no code, no waiting. You are signed in and dropped straight onto the scan screen.

Shortcut - If the invitation was sent to a Gmail address, tap Continue with Google on that same page. It does steps 2 to 4 in one tap, as long as the Google account you pick is the address the invitation was sent to.

Important - An invitation link works once and stops working seven days after it was created. A used or stale link cannot be revived; ask for a new one.

Path B

You are the first person from your company

Do this once per company. Everyone after you joins by invitation, not through this screen.

  1. Open RackTrack and tap Get Started.
  2. Fill in a @gmail.com email, a username, your Organization name (your company - this is what teammates will see), and a password, typed twice.
  3. Tap Continue. A six-digit code is sent to that inbox.
  4. Type the code into the six boxes and tap Verify. It expires after one minute - tap Resend if you miss it.
  5. You land on Waiting for approval. Your organization now exists, but it cannot scan yet.
  6. Message the RackTrack team and ask them to approve it. Then leave the screen open - it checks by itself every few seconds and lets you through the instant you are approved.

Why people get stuck here - Nobody is emailed when you sign up. The approval screen is not a queue that someone is watching; the RackTrack team only sees you by opening their console and looking. Sending them one message is almost always the whole fix.

Signing in from then on

  1. Leave the first box, Organization, empty. It is marked optional, and is only needed in the rare case where the same username exists in two organizations and RackTrack needs you to say which.
  2. Enter your username or email, then your password.
  3. Tap Sign in. Administrators land on the Organizations console; everyone else lands on the scan screen.

Good to know - You stay signed in for 30 days, so you will not be typing your password every visit. Losing signal does not sign you out either: RackTrack keeps working in the parts of a datacenter with no reception and syncs up again afterwards.

Roles - who can do what

Everybody in RackTrack has one role. It decides what appears in your menu and which scans you are allowed to see. To check your own, open Profile - it is listed there.

RoleWho this usually isWhat they can do
Member Most of your team. The people who go to the rack and scan it. Scan racks, read the results and correct them, reopen earlier scans, look up switch and firmware details, ask DOT for help, and edit their own profile.
Site Manager The person who runs one building or site. Everything a Member can do, and they look after the people at their own site: invite someone new, add an account by hand, switch a person off, or reset a person's password.
Org Admin The person who looks after your company's RackTrack account. Everything above, across every site in the company. They also create sites, connect your Data Sources such as a CMDB, and use the Marketplace.
Owner The RackTrack team, not somebody at your company. Runs the platform. Approves new companies and supports every organization.

Which scans each role can see

RoleScans visible to them
MemberTheir own site only.
Site ManagerTheir own site only. The same scans a Member at that site sees.
Org AdminEvery site in their own company.
OwnerEverything on the platform. This is the RackTrack team.

What you see in the menu - Everybody gets Home, Scan, 2 Racks, Ask DOT, Contact and Profile. An Org Admin also gets Organizations, Data Sources and Marketplace. The Owner additionally gets Console and Lab. If a colleague has menu items you do not have, it is their role that differs, not a fault.

What a Site Manager cannot do - Within their own site a Site Manager may switch a Member on or off and reset that Member's password. They cannot change a Member's username, email address, role or site, and they cannot manage another Site Manager.

One rule about Org Admins - Only the Owner can change an Org Admin account. One Org Admin cannot edit another, which keeps two administrators from locking each other out.

If you get stuck - find the words on your screen

What you seeWhat it actually meansWhat to do
Please use a @gmail.com email address to create an account You are on the sign-up screen with a work email. Only Gmail addresses can create an account unassisted. Do not fight it. Ask your administrator to invite your work address - invitations accept any domain.
Waiting for approval Your organization was created, but no one has approved it yet - and no one was told you are there. Message the RackTrack team. Leave the screen open; it lets you in by itself once approved.
Request not approved  /  Organization deactivated A person declined the request, or switched a previously active organization off. Contact the RackTrack team to discuss it. Nothing you tap in the app changes this.
The code never arrives, or it is rejected The code lives for one minute. It may also be sitting in spam, or the email was mistyped. Check spam, confirm the address, tap Resend, and enter the new code immediately.
This invite has already been used  /  This invite has expired Invitation links are single-use and die after seven days. Ask your administrator for a brand-new link. The old one cannot be revived.
This invite was sent to… but your Google account uses… You tapped Continue with Google and picked a different Google account from the invited address. Pick the account matching the invited address, or set a username and password on the page instead.
No RackTrack account uses… You tapped Continue with Google without an account. Google can sign you in or open an invitation - it cannot create a new organization. Open an invitation link, or use Path B with email and password.
rate_limited More than ten sign-in attempts in one minute. Nothing is broken and your account is not locked. Wait a full 60 seconds, then try once more.
You have simply forgotten the password No administrator needed - the sign-in screen handles this. Tap Forgot password?, enter your email, type the code, then choose No, take me to the app to get straight in without inventing a new password.

If you are the one inviting people

  1. Open the console, find the Site row the person belongs to, and tap Invite.
  2. Enter their email address and pick a role - Member or Site Manager. If you are a Site Manager yourself, every invitation you create is a Member: only an Org Admin can appoint a Site Manager.
  3. Tap Create invite link, then Copy.
  4. Send them the link yourself - email, chat, whatever actually reaches them.

The two rules that matter - RackTrack does not send the invitation for you: if you do not copy the link and send it, nothing reaches the person. And each link works once and expires after seven days.

Two other cases - For a shared account, a contractor, or someone with no reachable email, use + Add member instead: you set their username and password and hand them over. And if you see “Someone with that email already has an account”, they are already registered - send them to the sign-in screen, or to Forgot password?, rather than issuing an invitation.

Have ready before you start: the device you will scan from, the inbox for the address you are signing up with, and - if you were invited - the link itself. Everything in this guide describes the current build at demo.racktrack.ai. Anything not covered here is in the full RackTrack User Guide.
