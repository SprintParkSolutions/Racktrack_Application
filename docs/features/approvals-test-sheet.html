What to check, and what you should see

Thirty-seven checks in the order a real day goes. Each says what to do and what should happen, with the screen beside it. Every picture was taken on demo.racktrack.ai with these accounts and this data, so what you see should match. Tick them off; if a screen does not match its picture, quote the number.

What the person at the rack can do, and what they cannot.

A1

Do this. Sign in to the RackTrack app as meridian.tech and look at Scan.

You should see. The space you are standing in is chosen before the photo, so the scan is matched against that space's racks. Look at the menu: a technician has no Approvals entry.

A2

Do this. Open a scanned rack, then Report, then Drift vs NetBox.

You should see. It says how many things do not match and how many supporting records would be created on their own, and who the check will go to. One button: Send to the admin.

A3

Do this. Scroll the differences.

You should see. One line each, in the rack's words: "Not in NetBox". Ports are not listed separately; they belong to their device.

A4

Do this. Press Send to the admin, then look for anything that decides.

You should see. A "Track this check" link appears. There is no approve, assign or write on this screen, and the server refuses them to a technician even if the address is typed by hand.

Things that must be refused

Try to break it. Each of these should say no.

E1

Do this. Sign in as the owner account and open Approvals.

You should see. It shows only checks the owner raised itself, because a check belongs to an organization and the owner is in none. Do not use this account to test the workflow.

E2

Do this. As an admin of one organization, open a check from another by its address.

You should see. Not found. Nobody outside the organization can list or open it, the platform owner included.

E3

Do this. Leave a page open, have somebody deploy, then click something.

You should see. The page fetches itself once and carries on. If a real fault follows it stops and shows the message, with Reload the page beside Go to Home.

Known, and not worth writing up

Say so if you see these; they are understood.

  • A write can end with a few refusals. Some objects are already in NetBox under another key and are claimed rather than failed. What remains on this rack is two ports on one switch that the camera read with the same name; that clears when the rack is photographed again.
  • Alerts into Microsoft Teams are not built. The tokens do not exist yet. In the application and by email both work.
  • A large write takes a minute. The page follows it and shows the result when it lands.
  • The portal is a different product and has nothing to do with this. Nothing here goes near it.

If something does not match, give the number from this sheet, what you did and what you saw instead. That is enough to find it.
