RackTrack Demo Script

RackTrack demo script

Sign in as owner on the phone, stand at the office rack. Six steps, about eight minutes. Every screen shows where a fact came from; nothing is guessed.

1Scan the rack

Do

Scan, then photograph the rack front.

You see

Every device boxed with its U position, and the labels read off the front (SP-RI-U15-SW04).

From

The camera. Our own trained model finds devices and units; OCR reads the labels. Fewer than ten ports means a router; the rest are switches, patch panels, servers.

2Read the switches Network

Do

The three switches are already listed (D-Link core at .100, two TP-Links at .11 and .12). Tap Read.

You see

Each switch as a numbered card: make, model, serial, uptime, and every port in two rows, green where something is plugged in. Tap a port for its speed, the device on it and its addresses.

From

The switch itself, asked over SNMP by the phone on the rack's own network. Each switch is placed in the rack by matching its port count to the box the camera found; the place can be changed.

3What each switch is Switches

Do

Pick a switch.

You see

Firmware version and build, serial, address, ports; the specifications; the firmware check; SFP advice for its ports.

From

Identity and firmware from the switch's own MIB. Latest firmware from the maker's download list: TP-Link's Omada support site, D-Link's official firmware mirror. Behind a sign-in, the app says so and links the page. A make or model you type wins everywhere.

4The report More · Report

You see

One document for the rack: ports in use of the total, devices, switches read, cables, addresses, then every device top-down with its ports.

From

The camera and the switches joined. Download as CSV, JSON or PDF. Share to Teams or email, sent from the RackTrack mailbox with the PDF attached, or as a link that works for five minutes without an account.

5Export to NetBox

Do

Report, Export, NetBox, Preview, then Write.

You see

What will be created, updated and left alone; then the rack, devices, interfaces and cables appear in NetBox.

From

The same report. NetBox is provided for every account (Data Sources shows RackTrack NetBox · in use). An organisation can add its own with URL, username and password, and that one is used instead.

6Drift More · Drift

You see

What changed on each switch since the last read: ports that came up or went down, new devices, addresses that moved.

From

The readings the phone filed, compared over time.

If asked

  • Passwords on the switch? Read-only SNMP, a community or a v3 user, entered once per rack.
  • Is anything made up? No. Where a fact is not read, the app says it is not read.
  • Does the phone need the internet? Only for the firmware check, sharing and NetBox. Reading the switches works on the rack's network alone.
