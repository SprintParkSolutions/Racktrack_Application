RackTrack — Major Certifications, Policies & Documentation

A focused enterprise-readiness checklist containing only the major certifications, policies and documents RackTrack should prioritize.

FOCUSED CHECKLIST • AI SOFTWARE • ENTERPRISE READINESS

1. The Short Answer

For RackTrack, there is no need to maintain a huge list of unrelated certifications. The practical core is:

4 Major Certifications / Assurance Targets

ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27701 when applicable, and SOC 2 Type II when customers require it.

8 Major Policies

Security, access, privacy, AI governance, secure development, incident response, retention/deletion, and backup/DR.

11 Major Documents

Architecture, data flow, security, AI/model, dataset, validation, risk, security testing, API/deployment, DR and customer compliance documentation.

Recommended first focus: Build the policies and evidence first, then pursue ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as the two main certification targets.

2. Major Certifications & Assurance

PriorityCertification / AssurancePurposeRackTrack Action
PRIMARYISO/IEC 27001:2022Information Security Management System (ISMS).Primary certification target. Establishes a formal approach to information-security risk and controls. Official ISO reference
PRIMARYISO/IEC 42001:2023Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).Primary AI certification target. Establishes formal AI governance, risk management and lifecycle controls. Official ISO reference
CONDITIONALISO/IEC 27701:2025Privacy Information Management System.Consider when RackTrack's data-processing model and customers make formal privacy assurance valuable. Official ISO reference
CUSTOMER-DRIVENSOC 2 Type IIIndependent assurance over service-organization controls.Consider when US/global enterprise customers or procurement teams request SOC 2 evidence. AICPA Trust Services Criteria
Do not treat every item as mandatory. ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 are the main strategic targets. ISO 27701 and SOC 2 depend more strongly on RackTrack's data model, market and customer requirements.

3. Major Policies RackTrack Should Have

PolicyWhat it should define
1. Information Security PolicySecurity objectives, responsibilities, risk management, protection of company and customer information.
2. Access Control PolicyUser roles, least privilege, authentication, privileged access, access reviews and account lifecycle.
3. Data Protection & Privacy PolicyWhat data is collected, why it is processed, access, storage, sharing, retention and deletion.
4. AI Governance / Responsible AI PolicyAI inventory, intended use, risk assessment, human oversight, validation, monitoring and model changes.
5. Secure Software Development PolicySecure coding, code review, dependency management, secrets, testing and secure release practices.
6. Incident Response PolicySecurity-event detection, classification, escalation, containment, recovery, reporting and lessons learned.
7. Data Retention & Deletion PolicyRetention periods and secure deletion for images, reports, metadata, logs and backups.
8. Backup & Disaster Recovery PolicyBackup frequency, recovery objectives, restoration procedures, testing and responsibilities.

5. Core AI/ML Documentation

DocumentRequired Content

9. Enterprise Customer Compliance Pack

Keep one controlled package that can be provided to customers or procurement/security teams.

Security

Security overview, architecture, controls, encryption, access management, incident response and testing evidence.

AI

AI governance summary, model cards, validation reports, limitations and lifecycle controls.

Privacy

Privacy notice, data flow, retention, subprocessors and applicable DPA.

Product

Datasheet, architecture, deployment guide, API documentation and release process.

Operations

SLA, support process, backup/DR and business continuity information.

Certification

Actual certificates and audit/assurance reports only after they have been formally obtained.

10. Recommended Order of Implementation

1
Define scope and data.
Finalize architecture, deployment model, systems, teams and customer data handled by RackTrack.
2
Build the eight core policies.
Security, access, privacy, AI governance, secure development, incident response, retention/deletion and backup/DR.
3
Build security evidence.
Risk assessment, asset inventory, access reviews, logs, vulnerability testing, incident process and recovery tests.
4
Build AI evidence.
Dataset documentation, model cards, training records, frozen test set, validation metrics, failure analysis and model change records.
5
Complete privacy and software documentation.
Data map, retention, subprocessors, DPA where applicable, architecture, API, deployment, testing and release documents.
6
Perform a gap assessment.
Compare the complete evidence set against ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 requirements.
7
Pursue ISO/IEC 27001.
Use an appropriate independent certification body for the defined scope.
8
Pursue ISO/IEC 42001.
Formalize and independently assess the AI management system.
9
Add ISO/IEC 27701 or SOC 2 only when justified.
Use customer requirements, target markets and data-processing needs to decide.
10
Maintain continuously.
Update policies, risks, models, evidence, audits and certifications as RackTrack changes.
